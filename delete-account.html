Delete Your HabitRabbit Account

You can delete your HabitRabbit account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways to do this:

1. From inside the app

Open HabitRabbit and go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, habits, and history.

2. By email (if you no longer have the app installed)

Email habitrabbit.support@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and ask us to delete it. We will process your request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • All habits, completions, and progress data
  • Your app settings and preferences

What may be retained

Personal data is deleted within 30 days of your request. We may retain limited anonymized logs and records required by law (for example, for fraud prevention or legal compliance) for up to 90 days, after which they are also deleted. These records do not identify you personally.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Questions? Contact habitrabbit.support@gmail.com.
